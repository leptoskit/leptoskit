## How to maintain the record

Replace a proposed follow-up with its PR link and merged revision only after checking its actual result. Keep focused test counts separate from full gates and record opt-in journeys explicitly. A selector that skips all cases is not acceptance. If an environment deletes compiler inputs or runs out of disk, retain that failure and retry in a stable environment; do not re-record compiler snapshots to hide missing source snippets.
